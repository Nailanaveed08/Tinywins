# TinyWins 🪴

A calm, local-first habit tracker that celebrates showing up—not streaks.

> **TinyWins** helps you record small daily actions, notice what’s working, and keep your momentum without guilt or gamification pressure.

## Why it’s different

Most habit trackers make a missed day feel like failure. TinyWins focuses on **proof of effort**: log a win in seconds, add an optional note, and see your progress as a living garden. No streaks to protect. No account required. Your data stays on your device.

## Features

- 🌱 Add a win with one tap
- 🪴 Watch your garden grow as you log activity
- 📝 Add a short reflection to any day
- 📅 Browse a simple activity calendar
- 🔒 Local-first storage; no sign-up or tracking
- ♿ Designed for keyboard use and reduced motion

## Quick start

git clone https://github.com/Nailanaveed08/tinywins.git
cd tinywins

Open `index.html` in your browser. No build step or dependencies required.

## How it works

TinyWins is a tiny static web app. Entries are saved in your browser using `localStorage`; nothing is sent to a server. Export your data from **Settings → Export** before clearing browser data or switching devices.
